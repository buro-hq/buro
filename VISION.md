# Buro

Describe a useful result and let a team of agents work toward it in an isolated workspace. The team can inspect code, edit files, run commands, consult other agents and change models as its needs change.

Keep the system understandable. Tasks, attempts and conversations have separate identities. Reusable agents are instructions, without reserved names or hidden privileges. Review improves work through feedback and another iteration, without becoming a universal gate that prevents the team from continuing.

Keep defaults usable and overrides explicit. Do not require a project profile, predefined team or fixed workflow before someone can work. Make interruptions ordinary: retain history and files, explain what happened and allow a new attempt.

Keep the user in control of combining and publishing results. Completing an attempt saves what the agent produced. Applying changes, running a preview and publishing a PR remain separate actions.
